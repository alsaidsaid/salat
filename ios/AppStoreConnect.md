• تذكير يومي في الوقت الذي تختاره
• Widget للشاشة الرئيسية وشاشة القفل يعرض عددك
• يعمل بالكامل بلا إنترنت
• واجهة عربية مريحة للعين، مصمّمة للاستخدام الطويل

■ الخصوصية

لا يطلب التطبيق تسجيل دخول ولا اسماً ولا بريداً. عدد صلواتك محفوظ في جهازك وحده ولا يُرسل إلى أي خادم.

■ مجاني بالكامل

بلا إعلانات، وبلا اشتراكات، وبلا مشتريات داخل التطبيق.

اللهم صلِّ وسلّم على نبينا محمد.

## ٢. الحقول الأساسية — الإنجليزية

**App Name**
```
Salawat — Ibrahimi Counter
```

**Subtitle**
```
Blessings upon the Prophet ﷺ
```

**Keywords**
```
salawat,darood,durood,dhikr,tasbih,counter,islamic,muslim,azkar,blessings,sunnah
```

**Promotional Text**
```
A simple counter for the Ibrahimi salawat. Tap to move between the two phrases — every two taps count as one complete salawat. Free, no ads.
```

**Description**
```
A simple, calm counter for the Ibrahimi salawat upon the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.

The Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever sends one blessing upon me, Allah sends ten upon him" (Muslim).

■ HOW IT WORKS

The first phrase appears: "Allahumma salli ala Muhammad..."

Tap, and it moves to the second: "Allahumma barik ala Muhammad..."

Tap again and one complete salawat is counted, returning to the first phrase.

The Ibrahimi salawat is only complete with both phrases together — so every two taps count as one, not one per tap.

■ FEATURES

• Your count is saved and never lost when you close the app
• Remembers exactly which phrase you stopped at
• Haptic feedback and a soft tone on each tap
• A progress bar showing where you are between the two phrases
• Daily reminder at a time you choose
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets showing your count
• Works fully offline
• Arabic interface designed for long, comfortable sessions

■ PRIVACY

No sign-in, no name, no email. Your count stays on your device and is never sent to any server.

■ COMPLETELY FREE

No ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases.
```

## ٣. ملاحظات المراجعة (App Review Information)

**مهم جداً.** بدونها قد يظن المراجع أن العدّاد معطّل — لأنه لا يزيد إلا كل ضغطتين.

```
This app is a counter for the Ibrahimi salawat, an Islamic devotional phrase.

HOW TO TEST THE COUNTER:
The counter increments every TWO taps, not every tap. This is intentional: the
Ibrahimi salawat consists of two phrases ("salli" and "barik") and is only
complete when both have been recited. Tap 1 switches the phrase and the counter
stays at 0; tap 2 completes it and the counter becomes 1.

THIS VERSION HAS NO ADS AND NO IN-APP PURCHASES.
It is entirely free, contains no third-party SDKs, and makes no network
requests of any kind.

NO ACCOUNT IS REQUIRED. The app collects no personal data; the count is stored
only on device.
```
